# Voice recording — what to do

> **Status, 2026-10-03: pilot closed.** This sheet and `participant_scripts/` were used to collect the 12-speaker pilot corpus; collection is finished. They are kept as the record of what participants were asked and are not revised. Do not send them to anyone new without reading `RECORDING_PROTOCOL.md` section 1 (consent) first.


Thanks for helping. This takes **about 15 minutes**, you do it alone on your own
phone, and there is nothing to prepare.

---

## What this is

It is a final-year research project on **Tamil–English speech and voice-based
login**. We are testing whether a system can tell people apart from how they
talk. To do that we need recordings of people talking normally, in the mix of
Tamil and English they actually use.

You should be someone who **uses both Tamil and English day to day**. That is the
only requirement.

**What happens to your recording**

- Your audio is stored on the researcher's laptop, not on any website.
- A **voice model** is built from it and kept — not just the audio file.
- Your name is replaced with a code (like `spk_007`) everywhere in the project.
  Your name is never in the data, the paper, or any file we share.
- The research paper reports **averages across everyone**, never anything about
  you individually.
- Nobody outside the project team hears the recordings.

**One more thing to know now:** we will ask you for a **second, shorter recording
in about two weeks** (around 5 minutes). Comparing the two is the actual point of
the study, so please only say yes if you are willing to do both.

## Consent — reply with these before recording

Copy these three lines into your reply and put **yes** or **no** after each.

1. I agree to be recorded, to a voice model being built from my recording, and to
   both being used in this research project. — **yes / no**  *(required)*

2. I agree that my recording may be **altered to create fake or synthetic
   versions of my voice**, used only inside this project to test whether the
   system can detect them. — **yes / no**  *(optional; you can say no and still
   take part)*

3. I agree that my recording may later be **published as a public research
   dataset** for other researchers to use. — **yes / no**  *(optional; you can
   say no and still take part)*

You can withdraw at any time, without giving a reason. If you do, your audio,
your voice model and everything derived from it are deleted.

## How to record

**Where.** A quiet room with the door shut. Not a corridor, not outside, no fan
or AC directly overhead, no music, no TV.

**What with.** Your phone's normal voice recorder app. Hold it about a hand's
length from your mouth — not touching your face, not across the room.

**Four things that matter:**

- **One file per question.** Fourteen questions, fourteen separate files. Do not
  record it all as one long file.
- **Stay quiet for one second at the start** of each recording before you begin
  talking. Just press record, wait a beat, then speak. We need that bit of
  silence.
- **Do not convert the files or "clean" them.** Whatever your recorder saves is
  what we want. Re-saving them loses the thing we measure.
- **Speak the way you would to a friend.** Whatever comes out is right. Do
  **not** try to speak pure Tamil, and do **not** try to speak proper English.
  Switching between the two mid-sentence is completely normal and you do not
  need to think about it. There is no correct language here, and we are not
  checking your English or your Tamil.

Answer each question once, in one go. Please do not record a Tamil version and
an English version of the same answer — one natural answer is exactly what we
need.

If you fumble a sentence, just carry on. Do not re-record. Natural speech with
"um" and false starts is more useful to us than a clean take.

## What to say

**Fourteen questions. Aim for about 30 seconds on each one** — that is longer
than it feels, so keep talking until you have covered a few different things.
Under 10 seconds is too short for us to use.

Each question is given in English and Tamil. **Read it in whichever language you
like, then answer however comes naturally.** Do not answer in English just
because you read it in English.

---

**01 — about 30 seconds**
Tell me about the people you live with, and what they do.
நீங்க யாரோட வசிக்கறீங்க, அவங்க என்ன பண்றாங்க சொல்லுங்க.
*Cover a few people, not just one — who they are, what they do all day.*

**02 — about 30 seconds**
What did you eat yesterday, and who cooked it?
நேத்து என்ன சாப்பிட்டீங்க, யாரு சமைச்சாங்க?
*Go through the whole day — morning, afternoon, night.*

**03 — about 30 seconds**
Describe how you get here in the morning, start to finish.
காலையில இங்க எப்படி வர்றீங்கனு ஆரம்பத்துலருந்து சொல்லுங்க.
*From leaving the house to arriving. Every bus, stop and turn.*

**04 — about 30 seconds**
What did you spend money on last week, and roughly how much?
போன வாரம் எதுக்கு பணம் செலவழிச்சீங்க, எவ்வளவு?
*Four or five things, with the amounts. Guessing is fine.*

**05 — about 30 seconds**
Which apps do you use most on your phone, and what for?
உங்க ஃபோன்ல எந்த ஆப்ஸ் அதிகமா யூஸ் பண்றீங்க, எதுக்கு?
*Name a few, and say what you actually do in each.*

**06 — about 30 seconds**
Walk me through a normal college day.
ஒரு சாதாரண காலேஜ் நாள் எப்படி இருக்கும் சொல்லுங்க.
*Waking up to going to sleep.*

**07 — about 30 seconds**
How is Pongal celebrated in your house?
உங்க வீட்ல பொங்கல் எப்படி கொண்டாடுவீங்க?
*What is cooked, who comes, what everyone does.*

**08 — about 30 seconds**
Which places outside India would you like to see, and why?
இந்தியாவுக்கு வெளிய எந்த இடங்கள பார்க்கணும்னு இருக்கு, ஏன்?
*Two or three places, and the reason for each.*

**09 — about 30 seconds**
Tell me about the last film or series you watched.
கடைசியா பாத்த படம் அல்லது சீரிஸ் பத்தி சொல்லுங்க.
*What happened in it, and whether you liked it.*

**10 — about 20 seconds**
Say your year of birth. Then say what a plate of idli costs near you. Then say
what time you woke up today.
உங்க பிறந்த வருஷம் சொல்லுங்க. அப்புறம் உங்க ஏரியால ஒரு பிளேட் இட்லி விலை என்னன்னு
சொல்லுங்க. அப்புறம் இன்னைக்கு எத்தனை மணிக்கு எழுந்தீங்கன்னு சொல்லுங்க.
*Say them as full sentences, not just the three numbers.*

**11 — about 30 seconds**
What do you do when you fall sick?
உடம்பு சரியில்லைனா என்ன பண்ணுவீங்க?
*Who you tell, what you take, whether you go to a doctor.*

**12 — about 30 seconds**
Describe buying vegetables at the market — how much of what.
மார்க்கெட்ல காய்கறி வாங்குறத சொல்லுங்க — எவ்வளவு, எது.
*Which vegetables, what quantity, what it costs.*

**13 — about 30 seconds**
Tell me about your hometown and what it is known for.
உங்க ஊர் பத்தியும் அது எதுக்கு பேர் போனதுன்னும் சொல்லுங்க.
*Where it is, what it is famous for, what you like about it.*

**14 — about 5 seconds**
What is your mother's first name?
உங்க அம்மா பேர் என்ன?
*This one is meant to be short. Just answer it normally.*

## Sending it back

Name the files `01`, `02`, ... `14`, matching the question numbers. Put them all
in one folder or zip named with your first name, and send it to the researcher.

**Send them as files, not as voice messages.** If you use WhatsApp, attach them
using **Document**, not the microphone button and not the audio option — those
squash the recording and make it unusable for us. Google Drive, email or Telegram
are all fine.

If a file will not attach, tell us and we will find another way. Do not compress
or convert anything yourself.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any point, including halfway.
- You can withdraw later without giving a reason. Everything derived from your
  voice is deleted, not just the audio file.
- You can ask what we hold about you at any time.
- Your name is never stored with the recordings, and never appears in the paper.

Questions about any of this — ask before you record, not after.
